Three things we're testing

H1 · mechanism

Minmax produces fewer harmful outputs than KL alone.

H2 · detector bounds it

Harm reduction is capped by Detoxify's recall — self-harm is the expected blind spot.

H3 · the fix for H2

An activation probe should extend coverage to what Detoxify can't see.

Where the detector can't see

R_unsafe only fires when Detoxify's threshold is crossed. Fluent, clinical harm — self-harm language — never crosses it.
