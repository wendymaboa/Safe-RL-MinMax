Slide 8 — Reward hacking

The "Advertisements" exploit

"Advertisements" is one GPT-2 token. Detoxify scores it ≈ 0.001 toxicity.

Centered reward = 1 − 2×0.001 ≈ 0.998 — near maximum.

Minmax penalty only replaces reward when unsafe (tox > 0.3). "Advertisements" is always safe to Detoxify → R_unsafe never applies. The safety mechanism is optimised away.

Slide 19 — Claims

What we can vs cannot claim

Can claim

  • Detoxify-only reward is gameable (Advertisements exploit)
  • Weak KL (β=0.01) caused minmax policy collapse
  • Greedy eval misrepresented baseline under sample-trained policy
  • At β=0.2 + sample eval: minmax 2.0% vs baseline 3.0% vs raw 7.1% harm (1 seed)
  • Debugging trail documents ROSARL→LLM transfer difficulty

Cannot claim (yet)

  • Minmax beats baseline on safety in general (1 seed; baseline old env)
  • 0% harm from collapsed minmax = success
  • Self-calibration (Algorithm 1) is necessary vs fixed penalty — not tested
  • Proposal pilot harm reduction
  • Thesis-grade table without matched re-run in ppo-minmax
